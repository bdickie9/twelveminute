# Twelve Minute Desk — Scripts

Plain English. No “I hope this finds you well.”

## Kill — too vague

Thanks for reaching out. I’m not the right fit unless the job has a named outcome, a decision-maker on the thread, and a number already approved. If those three exist, reply with them in one note and I’ll tell you yes or no the same day.

## Kill — tire kicker after your range

That’s outside what I take on. I’ll pass. If the scope shrinks to [one sentence] and the budget is [number], I can look again. Otherwise good luck with it.

## Kill — they want a free audit / strategy

I don’t do unpaid audits. The paid version is [price] and includes [deliverable] in [days]. If you want that, say the word and I’ll send the invoice. If not, no hard feelings.

## Fence — price before work

I can do [outcome] for [price], prepaid, delivered by [date]. Anything outside that list is a change order. Reply YES and I’ll send the invoice. I don’t start on a maybe.

## After they go quiet for 5 days

Closing this thread. If the job is real, reply with the outcome, the signer, and the approved number. I’ll reopen it. Otherwise I’m out.

## After they try to negotiate hours

I don’t sell hours. The job is [outcome] for [price]. If that number doesn’t work, the honest move is to hire someone who bills time.

## When they want you to “just jump on a call first”

I can do a 15-minute call after you answer the two questions in writing: outcome in 30 days, and who signs with what number. Without that the call is a free consult and I don’t do those.
