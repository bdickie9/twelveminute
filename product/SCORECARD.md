# Twelve Minute Desk — Lead Scorecard

Print this or keep it as the first note in every inquiry. Fill it before you write a proposal.

Date:
Source:  referral / website / social / cold / other
Name / company:
Asked for:

## Score (0 or 1 each). Total out of 10.

[ ] They named a real outcome, not “need help with marketing/website/AI”
[ ] They named a deadline that is not “ASAP”
[ ] They have paid for this category of work before
[ ] A decision-maker is on the thread
[ ] Budget was offered or accepted when you stated a range
[ ] Scope is one job, not “be our person”
[ ] They answered your first two questions without a speech
[ ] No “just curious / shopping around / sending to my partner”
[ ] You can deliver this in writing without a new invention
[ ] You would take a second job from them

0–3  Kill. Send the polite no.
4–6  Maybe. Price at the top of your range. Prepay or kill.
7–10 Write the proposal. Time-box it to 25 minutes.

## The only two questions you ask in minute 1–3

1. What has to be true in 30 days for this to have been worth paying for?
2. Who signs and what number is already approved?

If either answer is fog, you are not late. You are being used as free consulting. Stop.
